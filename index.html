The System

One message. Four guards.

01

Sealed before it leaves

A Double Ratchet generates a brand-new key for every single message. Even Tandem can't read what you wrote.

02

Routed through Tor

Your phone hosts a hidden service. The message hops three Tor relays before it leaves your Wi-Fi.

03

Offline? A blind mailbox holds it

Mail waits in a mailbox addressed to a random token — not their identity. Sizes are padded to fixed buckets.

04

Delivered — honestly

The tick says what's true: sent, queued, delivered. No fake checkmarks. No silent losses.

On-Device AI

A security analyst living in your phone.

A transformer classifier — urlbert-tiny-v5 — runs locally via ONNX Runtime, backed by a deterministic rule engine.

Intercepted

Links are found inside messages the moment they arrive — nothing opens automatically.

Read locally

The AI model runs on-device. No cloud scanner sees where you bank, shop, or browse.

Judged in layers

AI probability + structural rules must agree before anything is flagged HIGH.

Honest, or silent

If the model can't run, the app says "Unable to verify this link." Failure is never dressed up as safe.
