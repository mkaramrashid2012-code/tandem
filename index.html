Sealed before it leaves

A Double Ratchet — built on libsodium — generates a brand-new key for every single message. Even Tandem can't read what you wrote.

Routed through Tor

Your phone hosts a hidden service. The message hops three Tor relays before it leaves your Wi-Fi — the network sees Tor, not you.

Offline? A blind mailbox holds it

If your contact is away, mail waits in a mailbox addressed to a random token — not their identity. Sizes are padded to fixed buckets. The relay can deliver, but can't profile.

Delivered — honestly

The tick says what's true: sent, queued, delivered. No fake checkmarks, no silent losses. When the link returns, delivery completes itself.

1 · Intercepted

2 · Read locally

The AI model runs on-device. No cloud scanner sees where you bank, shop, or browse.

3 · Judged in layers

AI probability + structural rules (shorteners, lookalike brands, punycode, raw IPs, executables) must agree before anything is flagged HIGH.

If the model can't run, the app says "Unable to verify this link." A failure is never dressed up as a safe result.

06 Privacy by architecture

Not a policy.
Physics.

No identity, by design

Offline mail is addressed to per-contact random tokens — the relay delivers without knowing who owns the mailbox. Mailboxes are created on first post and deleted after pickup.

Size leaks, sealed

Every message is padded to fixed size buckets and polling is jittered and shuffled — timing and length reveal nothing to cluster.

Nothing collected

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting. The only server involved is optional, blind, and can be run by you.

Verified, not vibes

Unit tests cover the crypto, the codec, the AI failure paths, and the padding ladder. The test log ships with the source.
